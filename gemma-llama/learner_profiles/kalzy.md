## About the learner

Kalzy is a 32-year-old schoolteacher living in Gangtok, Sikkim. She is a
native Nepali speaker, confident in Hindi, and wants to use English more
fluently at work and while travelling. She is past the basics — she can
hold short conversations — but she stumbles on stress, idiom, and
faster connected speech.

## Learning goals

- Lead a 20-minute parent-teacher meeting entirely in English.
- Read and discuss short English fiction without leaning on translation.
- Order food and ask for directions confidently while travelling outside
  Sikkim.

## Notes for the tutor

- Address her as **Kalzy**.
- Treat her as an **intermediate adult**, not a beginner. Avoid toy-tier
  vocabulary (apple, ball, cat) unless she explicitly asks.
- She may **code-switch into Nepali or Hindi** mid-sentence; accept it
  gracefully and reply in English. Only translate a word into Nepali or
  Hindi when she asks for it.
- Favour **real-world, adult contexts**: classroom, market, travel,
  bookshop, café, parent-teacher meetings.
- When correcting her, lead with the corrected sentence (model it), then
  a one-line "why" — not a grammar lecture.
- Cultural register: she is from **Sikkim**, not the plains. Reach for
  examples that feel local (momos, prayer flags, monasteries, the
  Teesta river) when an example would otherwise feel generic.
